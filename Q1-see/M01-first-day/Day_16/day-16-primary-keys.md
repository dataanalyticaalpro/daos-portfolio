# Day 16 – Primary Keys

## Objective

Understand why primary keys exist from a business perspective before learning their implementation in SQL.

## Key Learnings

- Businesses interact with many entities.
- Every business entity requires a unique identity.
- A table tells us what entity is being represented.
- A primary key tells us which specific entity it is.
- Businesses usually use surrogate keys because they remain stable over time.
- Primary keys identify entities, while foreign keys record relationships between entities by referencing those primary keys.

## Mental Model

Business
→ Business Process
→ Business Event
→ Business Entities Participate
→ Each Entity Needs a Unique Identity
→ Primary Key
