DAY 15 — BUSINESS PROCESSES CREATE RELATIONSHIPS

Data records evidence.
Entities store evidence.
Relationships exist because business entities interact through business processes.

Framework:

1. What business process connects these entities?
2. What event happened?
3. Which entities participated?
4. What evidence was created?

Think:

Business Process
        ↓
Business Event
        ↓
Entities Participate
        ↓
Data Is Recorded
        ↓
Tables Become Related

Important:

SQL JOIN does not create relationships.
It reconstructs the real-world business event recorded in the database.
